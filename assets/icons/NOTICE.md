Local technology tiles
======================

The SciPy, Apache Hive, Apache Flink, Metabase and Apache ECharts shapes derive from
[Simple Icons 16.34.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/16.34.0/icons).
Their vector paths are retained in `scripts/brand_paths.json`; color and background
treatment are local presentation changes. Simple Icons source licensing and icon
metadata are available in its repository. Brand names and logos belong to their
respective owners.

DataX, LightGBM, SHAP, Iceberg, Great Expectations, Uvicorn and openpyxl tiles use
original abbreviation artwork. They are labels, rather than official brand logos.

Other icons are embedded directly from Devicon 2.17.0, Skill Icons and TechStack
Generator, credited in the profile README. The wall represents project exposure
and optional experiments; it does not claim certification or skill ratings.
